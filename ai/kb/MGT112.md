## بنك الأسئلة المعتمد للمادة (الأجوبة الصحيحة مراجعة ومعتمدة، صفحته: https://www.asckwt.com/q/mgt112-k7x3/)
### Chapter 7: الهيكل والتصميم التنظيمي
- Which element of organizational design refers to dividing work into separate job tasks? [A) Departmentalization B) Work Specialization C) Authority D) Formalization] ✔ B) Work Specialization (تخصص العمل)
- Grouping employees based on customers’ problems and needs is called: [A) Product Departmentalization B) Customer Departmentalization C) Geographic Departmentalization D) Process Departmentalization] ✔ B) Customer Departmentalization (التقسيم حسب العملاء)
- The principle that each employee should report to only one manager is: [A) Span of Control B) Unity of Command C) Line Authority D) Staff Authority] ✔ B) Unity of Command (وحدة القيادة)
- Which type of power is based on fear? [A) Reward Power B) Coercive Power C) Expert Power D) Referent Power] ✔ B) Coercive Power (القوة القسرية)
- When decision-making is concentrated at upper levels, the organization is: [A) Decentralized B) Centralized C) Organic D) Mechanistic] ✔ B) Centralized (مركزية)
- Which factor increases the need for more supervision? [A) Employee training and experience B) Similarity of tasks C) Task complexity D) Informal communication] ✔ C) Task complexity (تعقيد المهام)
- A mechanistic organization is characterized by: [A) Few rules B) Informal communication C) Centralized authority D) Flatter structures] ✔ C) Centralized authority (سلطة مركزية)
- Which organizational design is most suitable for dynamic and uncertain environments? [A) Mechanistic B) Organic C) Functional D) Divisional] ✔ B) Organic (عضوي)
- Which traditional structure is fast, flexible, and inexpensive but risky as the organization grows? [A) Functional Structure B) Divisional Structure C) Simple Structure D) Matrix Structure] ✔ C) Simple Structure (الهيكل البسيط)
- Which contemporary design uses dual chains of command (functional + project)? [A) Team Structure B) Matrix Structure C) Project Structure D) Boundaryless Organization] ✔ B) Matrix Structure (الهيكل المصفوفي)
- Which structure focuses on results but increases duplication of activities and costs? [A) Functional B) Divisional C) Simple D) Team] ✔ B) Divisional (تقسيمي)
- Which design challenge involves keeping dispersed staff connected through technology? [A) Managing global structural issues B) Building a learning organization C) Designing flexible work arrangements D) Keeping employees connected] ✔ D) Keeping employees connected (الحفاظ على اتصال الموظفين)
- A learning organization is considered the only sustainable source of: [A) Competitive Advantage B) Centralization C) Formalization D) Departmentalization] ✔ A) Competitive Advantage (الميزة التنافسية)
- Which flexible work arrangement involves two or more people splitting one full-time job? [A) Flextime B) Job Sharing C) Telecommuting D) Compressed Workweek] ✔ B) Job Sharing (تقاسم الوظيفة)
- Employees working longer hours per day but fewer days per week is called: [A) Remote Work B) Compressed Workweek C) Flextime D) Contingent Workforce] ✔ B) Compressed Workweek (أسبوع عمل مضغوط)
### Chapter 8: إدارة الموارد البشرية
- Human Resource Management (HRM) is mainly concerned with: [A) Producing goods B) Getting, training, motivating, and keeping competent employees C) Financial planning D) Marketing strategies] ✔ B) Getting, training, motivating, and keeping competent employees (استقطاب وتدريب وتحفيز والاحتفاظ بالموظفين الأكفاء)
- Which step in the HRM process ensures the organization has the right number and kinds of people? [A) Employment Planning B) Orientation C) Compensation D) Performance Appraisal] ✔ A) Employment Planning (تخطيط التوظيف)
- Recruiting through employee referrals may reduce: [A) Costs B) Employee diversity C) Training needs D) Job satisfaction] ✔ B) Employee diversity (تنوع الموظفين)
- Which selection error occurs when a suitable applicant is rejected? [A) Accept Error B) Reject Error C) Reliability Error D) Validity Error] ✔ B) Reject Error (خطأ الرفض)
- Reliability in selection devices means: [A) Measuring the same thing consistently B) Relationship between test and job performance C) Predicting future demand D) Reducing recruitment costs] ✔ A) Measuring the same thing consistently (قياس الشيء نفسه بشكل ثابت)
- Which interview type is more effective in predicting job performance? [A) Traditional Interview B) Behavioral Interview C) Informal Interview D) Telephone Interview] ✔ B) Behavioral Interview (المقابلة السلوكية)
- A Realistic Job Preview (RJP) shows: [A) Only positive aspects B) Both positive and negative aspects C) Only negative aspects D) Salary details only] ✔ B) Both positive and negative aspects (الجوانب الإيجابية والسلبية معًا)
- Orientation programs mainly aim to: [A) Reduce employee anxiety B) Increase company profits C) Replace old employees D) Reduce training costs] ✔ A) Reduce employee anxiety (تقليل قلق الموظفين)
- Which training method involves learning by actually performing the job? [A) Job Rotation B) On-the-job Training C) Mentoring D) E-learning] ✔ B) On-the-job Training (التدريب أثناء العمل)
- Which performance appraisal method records effective and ineffective behaviors? [A) Written Essay B) Critical Incidents C) Adjective Rating Scales D) MBO] ✔ B) Critical Incidents (الحوادث الحرجة)
- 360-Degree Appraisal means: [A) Feedback only from managers B) Feedback from coworkers, managers, and others C) Ranking employees numerically D) Comparing employees in pairs] ✔ B) Feedback from coworkers, managers, and others (تغذية راجعة من الزملاء والمديرين وآخرين)
- Which factor usually leads to higher pay? [A) Short tenure B) Longer service and strong performance C) Low skill requirements D) Small company size] ✔ B) Longer service and strong performance (مدة خدمة أطول وأداء قوي)
- Unionized companies often provide: [A) Equal benefits to all workers B) Higher pay only to managers C) No benefits D) Flexible work arrangements] ✔ A) Equal benefits to all workers (مزايا موحدة لجميع العمال)
- Which contemporary HR issue involves respecting differences in culture, gender, and background? [A) Downsizing B) Workforce Diversity C) Sexual Harassment D) Workplace Spirituality] ✔ B) Workforce Diversity (تنوع القوى العاملة)
- Which HR issue requires managers to prevent unwanted behavior and protect employees? [A) Workforce Diversity B) Sexual Harassment C) Downsizing D) Compensation] ✔ B) Sexual Harassment (التحرش الجنسي)
