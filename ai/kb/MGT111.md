### Chapter 1: أساسيات الإدارة
- What is the final step in the decision-making process? [A) Implement the decision B) Evaluate decision effectiveness C) Select an alternative D) Develop alternatives] ✔ B) Evaluate decision effectiveness (تقييم فعالية القرار)
- The main characteristic of programmed decisions is: [A) Unique and nonrecurring B) Repetitive and routine C) Require creative solutions D) Involve high uncertainty] ✔ B) Repetitive and routine (متكررة وروتينية)
- Decision-making conditions include: [A) Certainty, Risk, Uncertainty B) Planning, Organizing, Leading C) Efficiency, Effectiveness, Productivity D) Innovation, Sustainability, Customers] ✔ A) Certainty, Risk, Uncertainty (التأكد، المخاطرة، عدم التأكد)
- Which bias means relying too much on first information? [A) Anchoring B) Availability C) Confirmation D) Hindsight] ✔ A) Anchoring (تحيز الارتساء (Anchoring))
- A manager who directs teachers and sets school policies is: [A) Top manager B) Middle manager C) First-line manager D) Team leader] ✔ A) Top manager (مدير في الإدارة العليا)
- Efficiency means: [A) Doing things right B) Doing the right things C) Achieving long-term goals D) Motivating employees] ✔ A) Doing things right (عمل الأشياء بالطريقة الصحيحة)
- Effectiveness means: [A) Doing things right B) Doing the right things C) Saving time and cost D) Supervising employees] ✔ B) Doing the right things (عمل الأشياء الصحيحة)
- Which decision-making approach is most realistic? [A) Rational B) Bounded rationality C) Intuitive D) Random] ✔ B) Bounded rationality (الرشد المحدود)
- Structured problems are: [A) New and unusual B) Clear and familiar C) Ambiguous D) Risky] ✔ B) Clear and familiar (واضحة ومألوفة)
- Nonprogrammed decisions are: [A) Routine B) Unique C) Repetitive D) Simple] ✔ B) Unique (فريدة)
- Which factor reshapes management today? [A) Customers B) Innovation C) Social media D) Sustainability E) All of the above] ✔ E) All of the above (كل ما سبق)
- Group decision-making advantage is: [A) Faster decisions B) More complete information C) Less time D) Clear responsibility] ✔ B) More complete information (معلومات أكثر اكتمالًا)
- Groupthink occurs when: [A) Members avoid expressing different opinions B) Leader is impartial C) Brainstorming is used D) Open discussion is encouraged] ✔ A) Members avoid expressing different opinions (يتجنب الأعضاء التعبير عن آراء مختلفة)
- Which skill helps managers analyze complex situations? [A) Technical B) Interpersonal C) Conceptual D) Political] ✔ C) Conceptual (المهارة المفاهيمية)
- Sustainability means: [A) Short-term profit B) Long-term value integration C) Avoiding innovation D) Ignoring customers] ✔ B) Long-term value integration (دمج القيمة على المدى الطويل)
- Managers only work in profit organizations. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- Rational decision-making is always realistic. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- Efficiency means doing things right with minimum cost and time. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Effectiveness means completing the right tasks that achieve goals. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Group decision-making always saves time. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- National culture influences how decisions are made. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
### Chapter 2: اتخاذ القرار
- What is the first step in the decision-making process? [A) Identifying decision criteria B) Problem identification C) Developing alternatives D) Implementing the decision] ✔ B) Problem identification (تحديد المشكلة)
- Which step involves ranking criteria by importance? [A) Developing alternatives B) Weighting criteria C) Selecting the best alternative D) Evaluating the decision] ✔ B) Weighting criteria (إعطاء أوزان للمعايير)
- Which bias means choosing quick rewards over long-term benefits? [A) Overconfidence B) Immediate gratification C) Availability D) Anchoring] ✔ B) Immediate gratification (الإشباع الفوري)
- Which bias occurs when managers fail to update decisions with new information? [A) Revision B) Confirmation C) Framing D) Representation] ✔ A) Revision (تحيز المراجعة (Revision))
- Rational decision-making assumes: [A) Complete objectivity and logic B) Limited information processing C) Decisions based on intuition D) Decisions based on culture] ✔ A) Complete objectivity and logic (موضوعية ومنطق كاملين)
- Bounded rationality means: [A) Managers are fully logical B) Managers are limited in processing information C) Managers rely only on emotions D) Managers ignore alternatives] ✔ B) Managers are limited in processing information (المدراء محدودين في معالجة المعلومات)
- Intuitive decision-making is based on: [A) Past experience and feelings B) Only data analysis C) Strict rules and policies D) Randomness] ✔ A) Past experience and feelings (الخبرة السابقة والمشاعر)
- Structured problems are: [A) New and unusual B) Clear and familiar C) Ambiguous D) Risky] ✔ B) Clear and familiar (واضحة ومألوفة)
- Nonprogrammed decisions are: [A) Routine and repetitive B) Unique and nonrecurring C) Simple and clear D) Based on rules] ✔ B) Unique and nonrecurring (فريدة وغير متكررة)
- Which guide is used for programmed decisions? [A) Creativity B) Procedures, rules, policies C) Intuition D) Brainstorming] ✔ B) Procedures, rules, policies (الإجراءات والقواعد والسياسات)
- Decision-making under certainty means: [A) Outcomes are known B) Outcomes are estimated C) Outcomes are unclear D) Outcomes are random] ✔ A) Outcomes are known (النتائج معروفة)
- Decision-making under risk means: [A) Outcomes are known with certainty B) Probabilities can be estimated C) No information is available D) Decisions are intuitive only] ✔ B) Probabilities can be estimated (يمكن تقدير الاحتمالات)
- Decision-making under uncertainty means: [A) Probabilities are clear B) Outcomes are unknown C) Outcomes are guaranteed D) Decisions are routine] ✔ B) Outcomes are unknown (النتائج غير معروفة)
- Which is an advantage of group decision-making? [A) Faster decisions B) More alternatives generated C) Clearer responsibility D) Less time-consuming] ✔ B) More alternatives generated (توليد بدائل أكثر)
- Which contemporary issue involves analyzing vast amounts of information? [A) National culture B) Creativity C) Design thinking D) Big Data] ✔ D) Big Data (البيانات الضخمة)
- Overconfidence bias means being too confident in your abilities. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Rational decision-making is always realistic in practice. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- Programmed decisions are repetitive and routine. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Nonprogrammed decisions require unique solutions. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Groupthink improves the quality of decisions. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- Design thinking approaches management problems like design problems. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
