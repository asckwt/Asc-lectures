### Chapter 1: الوسائط الرقمية
- Which of the following is a benefit of printed messages? [A) Faster communication B) Permanent, unchangeable record C) Easy to edit anytime D) Less formal impression] ✔ B) Permanent, unchangeable record (سجل دائم وما يتغير)

- What is considered the most important part of an email message? [A) Greeting B) Subject line C) Signature D) Tone] ✔ B) Subject line (سطر الموضوع)
- Which feature of workgroup messaging systems helps organize teamwork? [A) Threads B) Channels C) Emojis D) Podcasts] ✔ B) Channels (القنوات (Channels))
- Which is a risk of using emojis in professional communication? [A) Inconsistent interpretations B) Faster communication C) Stronger relationships D) Clearer tone] ✔ A) Inconsistent interpretations (اختلاف تفسيرها من شخص لشخص)
- Which compositional mode uses storytelling with beginning, middle, and end? [A) Summaries B) Narratives C) Teasers D) Reference material] ✔ B) Narratives (السرد (Narratives))
- Which guideline for email writing emphasizes clarity and meaning? [A) Greeting B) Subject line C) emoji D) Signature] ✔ B) Subject line (سطر الموضوع)
- Which AI feature helps summarize long email threads? [A) Sentiment analysis B) Thread management C) Summarization D) Prioritization] ✔ C) Summarization (التلخيص)
- Which messaging platform is commonly used for collaboration? [A) Slack B) TikTok C) Instagram D) Pinterest] ✔ A) Slack (سلاك (Slack))
- Which compositional mode is best for short posts that inform readers of updates? [A) Status updates & announcements B) Narratives C) Teasers D) Comments] ✔ A) Status updates & announcements (تحديثات الحالة والإعلانات)
- Which is a challenge in organizing website content? [A) Multiple audiences B) Easy planning C) Simple organization D) Limited purposes] ✔ A) Multiple audiences (تعدد الجماهير)
- Which podcasting application helps HR attract new recruits? [A) Training B) Marketing C) HR/Recruiting D) Selling] ✔ C) HR/Recruiting (الموارد البشرية والتوظيف)
- Which step in the three-step podcasting process involves recording and distributing? [A) Plan B) Write C) Complete D) Organize] ✔ C) Complete (الإكمال (Complete))
- Which is a best practice for business messaging? [A) Use channels and threads B) Avoid company policies C) Write long complex messages D) Ignore tone] ✔ A) Use channels and threads (استخدام القنوات والمحادثات المتسلسلة)
- Which benefit of printed messages makes them stand out? [A) Easy to edit B) Less formal impression C) Standing out from digital flood D) Faster communication] ✔ C) Standing out from digital flood (التميّز وسط زحمة المحتوى الرقمي)
- Emojis should always be used in formal communication. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- Subject lines are the most important part of an email. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Narratives are short versions of documents highlighting key points. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- Slack and Teams are examples of workgroup messaging systems. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Printed messages can provide a permanent, secure record. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Podcasting can be used for training and marketing. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)

- وصّل: Orientations (التوجيهات) = Guidance that helps readers navigate new topics

- وصّل: Narratives (السرد) = Storytelling with beginning, middle, end
- وصّل: Teasers (التشويق) = Withholding info to attract attention
- وصّل: Status Updates (تحديثات الحالة) = Short posts that inform readers
