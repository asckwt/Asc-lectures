### Chapter 4: إدارة المعلومات التسويقية
- What is Big Data? [A) Small customer notes B) Large volume of marketing data C) Company profits D) Advertising campaigns] ✔ B) Large volume of marketing data (حجم كبير من البيانات التسويقية)
- Why do companies need marketing information? [A) To understand customer needs B) To reduce costs only C) To ignore competitors D) To stop promotions] ✔ A) To understand customer needs (لفهم احتياجات العملاء)
- Which is NOT a source of marketing information? [A) Internal databases B) Marketing intelligence C) Marketing research D) Customer gossip only] ✔ D) Customer gossip only (إشاعات العملاء فقط)
- What is the first step in the marketing research process? [A) Developing the plan B) Defining the problem & objectives C) Implementing the plan D) Reporting findings] ✔ B) Defining the problem & objectives (تحديد المشكلة والأهداف)
- Which type of research describes market situations? [A) Exploratory B) Descriptive C) Causal D) Experimental] ✔ B) Descriptive (وصفي)
- Which is an advantage of secondary data? [A) Lower cost B) Always accurate C) Always current D) Always impartial] ✔ A) Lower cost (تكلفة أقل)
- What do CRM platforms provide? [A) A complete view of customers B) Only sales reports C) Only advertising data D) Only competitor info] ✔ A) A complete view of customers (رؤية شاملة للعملاء)
- The marketing information ecosystem (MIE) is a system that: [A) Identifies information needs, develops information, and helps managers use it B) Only stores sales records C) Sets product prices D) Designs advertising campaigns] ✔ A) Identifies information needs, develops information, and helps managers use it (يحدد احتياجات المعلومات ويطورها ويساعد المدراء على استخدامها)
- A good marketing information system provides: [A) As much information as possible B) The right amount of information, neither too much nor too little C) Only information about competitors D) Information only to external partners] ✔ B) The right amount of information, neither too much nor too little (الكمية المناسبة من المعلومات، لا كثيرة ولا قليلة)
- Which source can usually be accessed more quickly and cheaply than other sources? [A) Marketing research B) Internal databases C) Focus groups D) Ethnographic research] ✔ B) Internal databases (قواعد البيانات الداخلية)
- The systematic collection and analysis of publicly available information about consumers and competitors is: [A) Competitive marketing intelligence B) Internal databases C) Causal research D) Sampling plan] ✔ A) Competitive marketing intelligence (الذكاء التسويقي التنافسي)
- Which research type gathers preliminary information to help define the problem and suggest hypotheses? [A) Exploratory B) Descriptive C) Causal D) Experimental] ✔ A) Exploratory (البحث الاستكشافي)
- Which research type tests hypotheses about cause-and-effect relationships? [A) Exploratory B) Descriptive C) Causal D) Observational] ✔ C) Causal (البحث السببي)
- Information collected for the specific purpose at hand is called: [A) Secondary data B) Primary data C) Big Data D) Internal data] ✔ B) Primary data (البيانات الأولية)
- Which is a disadvantage of secondary data? [A) Lower cost B) Obtained quickly C) Data may not be current D) Easy to find] ✔ C) Data may not be current (ممكن ما تكون حديثة)
- Trained observers studying consumers in their natural settings, such as visiting families at home, is: [A) Survey research B) Ethnographic research C) Experimental research D) Telephone interviews] ✔ B) Ethnographic research (البحث الإثنوغرافي)
- Testing different ads with two groups to compare the effects is: [A) Observational research B) Survey research C) Experimental research D) Ethnographic research] ✔ C) Experimental research (البحث التجريبي)
- Which contact method is low-cost and gets honest answers, but is slow with a low response rate? [A) Mail questionnaires B) Telephone interviews C) Personal interviews D) Focus groups] ✔ A) Mail questionnaires (الاستبيانات البريدية)
- What is the last step in the marketing research process? [A) Defining the problem B) Developing the research plan C) Implementing the research plan D) Interpreting and reporting the findings] ✔ D) Interpreting and reporting the findings (تفسير النتائج وإعداد التقرير)
- Starbucks’ “Deep Brew” program is an example of using: [A) Artificial intelligence B) Mail questionnaires C) Focus groups D) Secondary data only] ✔ A) Artificial intelligence (الذكاء الاصطناعي)
- Companies should balance the value of information with the cost of obtaining it. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- The marketing information system provides information only to the company's managers and never to external partners. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- Secondary data is information that already exists, collected for another purpose. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Focus groups are small group discussions led by a trained interviewer. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Telephone interviews are slow and inflexible. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- Consumer privacy is one of the key ethical issues in marketing research. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
### Chapter 5: أسواق المستهلكين وسلوك المشتري
- What is consumer buyer behavior? [A) Buying for resale B) Buying for personal use C) Buying for company use D) Buying for export] ✔ B) Buying for personal use (الشراء للاستهلاك الشخصي)
- Which factor includes culture and social class? [A) Social B) Cultural C) Personal D) Psychological] ✔ B) Cultural (ثقافي)
- Word-of-mouth influence means… [A) Ads only B) Recommendations from trusted people C) Company promotions D) Discounts] ✔ B) Recommendations from trusted people (توصيات من أشخاص موثوقين)
- Which personal factor affects buying behavior? [A) Occupation B) Family C) Culture D) Motivation] ✔ A) Occupation (الوظيفة)
- Which psychological factor explains selective attention? [A) Motivation B) Perception C) Learning D) Beliefs] ✔ B) Perception (الإدراك)
- In Maslow’s hierarchy, which need comes first? [A) Esteem B) Social C) Physiological D) Self-actualization] ✔ C) Physiological (فسيولوجي)
- Which type of buying behavior shows high involvement + big differences? [A) Complex B) Habitual C) Variety-seeking D) Dissonance-reducing] ✔ A) Complex (معقد)
- Which product characteristic means “easy to try”? [A) Relative advantage B) Compatibility C) Trialability D) Observability] ✔ C) Trialability (إمكانية التجربة)
