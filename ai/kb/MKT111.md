### Chapter 1: خلق قيمة للعميل وتعزيز تفاعله
- What is marketing? [A) Selling products only B) Building relationships and creating value C) Advertising only D) Reducing costs] ✔ B) Building relationships and creating value (بناء علاقات وخلق قيمة)
- Which is NOT a step in the marketing process? [A) Understand customer needs B) Design value-driven strategy C) Build marketing mix D) Ignore customer satisfaction] ✔ D) Ignore customer satisfaction (تجاهل رضا العملاء)
- Needs are… [A) Basic human requirements B) Shaped by culture C) Backed by buying power D) Company profits] ✔ A) Basic human requirements (متطلبات إنسانية أساسية)
- Marketing Myopia means… [A) Focusing only on product B) Focusing on customer benefits C) Focusing on competitors D) Focusing on profits] ✔ A) Focusing only on product (التركيز فقط على المنتج)
- Which concept balances customer wants, company needs, and society’s interests? [A) Production concept B) Product concept C) Marketing concept D) Societal marketing] ✔ D) Societal marketing (التسويق المجتمعي)
- Wants are: [A) Basic physical needs B) The form needs take as shaped by culture and personality C) Wants backed by buying power D) Products offered to a market] ✔ B) The form needs take as shaped by culture and personality (الشكل اللي تاخذه الاحتياجات حسب الثقافة والشخصية)
- Demands are human wants that are: [A) Shaped by culture B) Backed by buying power C) Basic physical needs D) Offered by competitors] ✔ B) Backed by buying power (مدعومة بالقوة الشرائية)
- A market is: [A) The set of actual and potential buyers of a product B) Only current customers C) A company's sales team D) A list of competitors] ✔ A) The set of actual and potential buyers of a product (مجموعة المشترين الفعليين والمحتملين للمنتج)
- Exchange is: [A) Obtaining a desired object by offering something in return B) Giving products for free C) Advertising a product D) Lowering prices] ✔ A) Obtaining a desired object by offering something in return (الحصول على شيء مرغوب مقابل تقديم شيء آخر)
- Which concept says consumers favor available and highly affordable products? [A) Production concept B) Product concept C) Selling concept D) Marketing concept] ✔ A) Production concept (مفهوم الإنتاج)
- Which concept says consumers won't buy enough unless the firm undertakes large-scale selling and promotion? [A) Product concept B) Selling concept C) Marketing concept D) Societal marketing] ✔ B) Selling concept (مفهوم البيع)
- A brand's value proposition is: [A) Its list price B) The set of benefits it promises to deliver to customers C) Its advertising budget D) Its number of stores] ✔ B) The set of benefits it promises to deliver to customers (مجموعة الفوائد اللي تعد بتقديمها للعملاء)
- Customer satisfaction depends on: [A) The product's perceived performance relative to the buyer's expectations B) The product's price only C) The number of competitors D) The company's profits] ✔ A) The product's perceived performance relative to the buyer's expectations (الأداء المدرك للمنتج مقارنة بتوقعات المشتري)
- Customers who are potentially profitable but not loyal are called: [A) Strangers B) Butterflies C) True friends D) Barnacles] ✔ B) Butterflies (الفراشات (Butterflies))
- Customer equity is: [A) The total combined customer lifetime values of all the company's customers B) The share of one customer's purchases C) The company's total assets D) The price of a product] ✔ A) The total combined customer lifetime values of all the company's customers (مجموع قيم مدى الحياة لكل عملاء الشركة)
- Share of customer is: [A) The portion of the customer's purchasing a company gets in its product categories B) The number of customers in a market C) The value of a customer's purchases over a lifetime D) The company's market share] ✔ A) The portion of the customer's purchasing a company gets in its product categories (الجزء من مشتريات العميل اللي تحصل عليه الشركة ضمن فئات منتجاتها)
- Brand exchanges created by consumers themselves are called: [A) Partner relationship management B) Consumer-generated marketing C) Mobile marketing D) Societal marketing] ✔ B) Consumer-generated marketing (التسويق اللي ينشئه المستهلك)
- Satisfied customers are likely to buy again. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Barnacles are highly profitable but not loyal. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- The four Ps of the marketing mix are product, price, place, and promotion. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Customer lifetime value is the value of a single purchase. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- Not-for-profit organizations can use marketing to attract members, funds, and support. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Mobile marketing uses mobile channels to stimulate immediate buying. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
### Chapter 2: استراتيجية الشركة والتسويق
- What is the first step in strategic planning? [A) Setting objectives B) Mission statement C) Designing portfolio D) Marketing mix] ✔ B) Mission statement (بيان المهمة)
- In the BCG Matrix, “Stars” are… [A) High growth, high share B) Low growth, high share C) High growth, low share D) Low growth, low share] ✔ A) High growth, high share (نمو مرتفع، حصة مرتفعة)
- Market penetration means… [A) Selling more to current customers B) Entering new markets C) Creating new products D) Diversifying business] ✔ A) Selling more to current customers (بيع أكثر للعملاء الحاليين)
- Market positioning is… [A) Dividing customers B) Choosing target segments C) Creating a clear image in customer’s mind D) Offering discounts] ✔ C) Creating a clear image in customer’s mind (إنشاء صورة واضحة في ذهن العميل)
- The marketing mix (4Ps) includes… [A) Product, Price, Place, Promotion B) People, Process, Profit, Planning C) Needs, Wants, Demands, Value D) Segmentation, Targeting, Positioning, Differentiation] ✔ A) Product, Price, Place, Promotion (المنتج، السعر، المكان، الترويج)
- Strategic planning is the process of developing and maintaining a strategic fit between: [A) The organization's goals and capabilities and its changing marketing opportunities B) Prices and costs C) Managers and employees D) Products and packaging] ✔ A) The organization's goals and capabilities and its changing marketing opportunities (أهداف المنظمة وقدراتها وفرصها التسويقية المتغيرة)
- A mission statement should NOT be: [A) Meaningful and specific B) Motivating C) Stated as making sales or profits D) Focused on the company's strengths] ✔ C) Stated as making sales or profits (مكتوب على شكل تحقيق مبيعات أو أرباح)
- In the BCG matrix, low-growth, high-share businesses are called: [A) Stars B) Cash cows C) Question marks D) Dogs] ✔ B) Cash cows (البقرة الحلوب (Cash cows))
- Question marks are: [A) Low-share units in high-growth markets B) High-share units in low-growth markets C) High-growth, high-share units D) Low-growth, low-share units] ✔ A) Low-share units in high-growth markets (وحدات حصتها قليلة في أسواق نموها عالي)
- Dogs in the BCG matrix are: [A) High growth, high share B) Low growth, high share C) High growth, low share D) Low growth, low share] ✔ D) Low growth, low share (نمو منخفض، حصة منخفضة)
- Offering modified or new products to current markets is: [A) Market penetration B) Market development C) Product development D) Diversification] ✔ C) Product development (تطوير المنتج)
- Identifying and developing new markets for current products is: [A) Market penetration B) Market development C) Product development D) Diversification] ✔ B) Market development (تطوير السوق)
- Starting up or buying businesses beyond current products and markets is: [A) Market penetration B) Market development C) Product development D) Diversification] ✔ D) Diversification (التنويع)
- The value chain is: [A) A series of departments that carry out value-creating activities B) A group of competitors C) A list of product prices D) A type of advertising] ✔ A) A series of departments that carry out value-creating activities (سلسلة أقسام تقوم بأنشطة تخلق القيمة)
- Evaluating each segment's attractiveness and selecting one or more segments to enter is: [A) Market segmentation B) Market targeting C) Market positioning D) Differentiation] ✔ B) Market targeting (استهداف السوق)
- Which marketing management function turns plans into real actions? [A) Analysis B) Planning C) Implementation D) Control] ✔ C) Implementation (التنفيذ)
- In SWOT analysis, internal limitations that may interfere with reaching objectives are: [A) Strengths B) Weaknesses C) Opportunities D) Threats] ✔ B) Weaknesses (نقاط الضعف)
- If a company spends $1,000 on ads and earns $3,000, the marketing ROI is: [A) 100% B) 200% C) 300% D) 30%] ✔ B) 200% (٢٠٠٪)
- Stars require heavy investment and eventually turn into cash cows. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Marketing alone can create superior customer value. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- A strategic business unit can be a company division, a product line, or a single product or brand. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Matrix approaches are quick and inexpensive. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- Threats are internal factors that may challenge a company's performance. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- The marketing mix tools are controllable and tactical. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
