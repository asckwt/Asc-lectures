### Chapter 1: التواصل المهني في عصر الذكاء الاصطناعي
- One result of poor communication in operations is: [A) Better teamwork B) Higher customer trust C) Mistakes and delays D) Faster production] ✔ C) Mistakes and delays (الأخطاء والتأخير)
- A practical message should: [A) Help the reader solve a problem B) Make the reader feel happy C) Include unrelated facts D) Be as long as possible] ✔ A) Help the reader solve a problem (تساعد القارئ على حل مشكلة)
- Improving your communication skills is: [A) The single most important step in your career B) Not important if you work with numbers C) A minor component of success D) Unnecessary for entrepreneurs] ✔ A) The single most important step in your career (أهم خطوة في مسيرتك المهنية)
- Focusing on _________ makes communication more effective: [A) Clarifying expectations and responsibilities B) Opinions C) Personal goals D) Impressions] ✔ A) Clarifying expectations and responsibilities (توضيح التوقعات والمسؤوليات)
- Companies value diversity today mainly because: [A) Equal opportunity B) Prevent lawsuits C) Competitive advantages D) Reduce risk] ✔ C) Competitive advantages (المزايا التنافسية)
- Which of the following is NOT a form of unethical communication? [A) Withholding information B) Distorting information C) Plagiarizing D) Transparency] ✔ D) Transparency (الشفافية)
- Which communication direction flows from employees to managers? [A) Downward B) Upward C) Horizontal D) Diagonal] ✔ B) Upward (التواصل الصاعد)
- Emotional intelligence helps you: [A) Understand and relate to others B) Avoid technology C) Focus only on tasks D) Ignore emotions] ✔ A) Understand and relate to others (فهم الآخرين والتواصل معهم)
- Generative AI is capable of: [A) Only analyzing data B) Producing text, images, audio, video C) Managing employees D) Writing contracts] ✔ B) Producing text, images, audio, video (إنتاج النصوص والصور والصوت والفيديو)
- Which is a risk of GenAI? [A) Mistakes and hallucinations B) Faster communication C) Better personalization D) Improved collaboration] ✔ A) Mistakes and hallucinations (الأخطاء والهلوسة)
- Which skill involves analyzing situations and making sound judgments? [A) Collaboration B) Critical Thinking C) Data Literacy D) Business Ethics] ✔ B) Critical Thinking (التفكير النقدي)
- Which of the following is part of legal communication? [A) Gossip B) Contracts C) Informal chats D) Personal bias] ✔ B) Contracts (العقود)
- Which communication network is informal? [A) Downward B) Horizontal C) Informal D) Diagonal] ✔ C) Informal (الشبكة غير الرسمية)
- Which guideline ensures ethical communication? [A) Include all relevant information B) Hide sensitive details C) Distort facts D) Ignore consequences] ✔ A) Include all relevant information (تضمين كل المعلومات ذات الصلة)
- Which is a benefit of AI in communication? [A) Personalization and localization B) Erosion of human connections C) Overreliance on tools D) Ethical lapses] ✔ A) Personalization and localization (التخصيص والتوطين)
- Effective communication builds strong ties with stakeholders. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Upward communication flows from managers to employees. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- Generative AI can never make mistakes. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- Transparency is the practice of being open and honest, giving the audience complete access to the information they need to understand a message correctly. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Plagiarizing is considered ethical. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- Data literacy means accessing and interpreting data accurately. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- وصّل: Formal Communication (التواصل الرسمي) = Ideas flow along company structure
- وصّل: Downward Communication (التواصل الهابط) = From managers to employees
- وصّل: Upward Communication (التواصل الصاعد) = From employees to managers
- وصّل: Horizontal Communication (التواصل الأفقي) = Between departments for teamwork
- وصّل: Diagonal Communication (التواصل القُطري) = Across different levels/departments
- وصّل: Informal Network (الشبكة غير الرسمية) = Personal conversations inside company
### Chapter 2: التعاون والتواصل الشخصي وإتيكيت الأعمال
- Which stage of team development involves conflict? [A) Forming B) Storming C) Norming D) Performing] ✔ B) Storming (مرحلة العصف (Storming))
- Which is a characteristic of effective teams? [A) Lack of purpose B) Psychological safety C) Ignoring communication D) Undefined goals] ✔ B) Psychological safety (الأمان النفسي)
- A virtual team is defined as: [A) Members in one office B) Members in different locations using technology C) Informal group chats D) Temporary social groups] ✔ B) Members in different locations using technology (أعضاء في مواقع مختلفة يستخدمون التكنولوجيا)
- Which is NOT a guideline for collaborative writing? [A) Clear goals B) Frequent checkpoints C) Ignoring writing styles D) Using strengths] ✔ C) Ignoring writing styles (تجاهل أساليب الكتابة)
- Constructive feedback should: [A) Focus on flaws only B) Be specific and impersonal C) Be destructive D) Ignore timing] ✔ B) Be specific and impersonal (تكون محددة وغير شخصية)
- Which step is essential for productive meetings? [A) Define purpose B) Avoid agenda C) Invite everyone D) Skip preparation] ✔ A) Define purpose (تحديد الهدف)
- Which listening type evaluates logic and evidence? [A) Content listening B) Critical listening C) Empathic listening D) Active listening] ✔ B) Critical listening (الاستماع النقدي)
- Which listening type focuses on feelings and needs? [A) Empathic listening B) Critical listening C) Content listening D) Defensive listening] ✔ A) Empathic listening (الاستماع التعاطفي)
- Which is a major category of nonverbal communication? [A) Facial expressions B) Contracts C) Written reports D) Emails] ✔ A) Facial expressions (تعابير الوجه)
- Which etiquette rule is essential in meetings? [A) Respect time B) Gossip C) Eating at desk D) Ignoring hygiene] ✔ A) Respect time (احترام الوقت)
- Business casual attire includes: [A) Jeans and T-shirts B) Collared shirts and slacks C) Suits and ties D) Formal dresses] ✔ B) Collared shirts and slacks (قمصان بياقة وبناطيل قماش)
- Which is NOT part of virtual workplace etiquette? [A) Avoid personal attacks B) Stay focused C) Use virus protection D) Gossip] ✔ D) Gossip (النميمة)
- Which is a guideline for online etiquette? [A) Assume privacy B) Think before you post C) Waste others’ time D) Ignore spelling] ✔ B) Think before you post (فكّر قبل لا تنشر)
- Which is a mobile etiquette rule? [A) Keep private calls private B) Record without consent C) Loud ringtones in meetings D) Ignore nonverbal signals] ✔ A) Keep private calls private (خلّ مكالماتك الخاصة خاصة)
- Which is a business etiquette rule in social settings? [A) Represent your company B) Ignore customs C) Choose alcohol D) Avoid introductions] ✔ A) Represent your company (تمثيل شركتك)
- Norming stage is when team rules become clear. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Hybrid teams include both co-located and remote members. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Constructive feedback should highlight flaws only. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- Effective meetings require a clear agenda. [A) True B) False] ✔ A) True (صح)
- Empathic listening focuses on logic and evidence. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- Business formal attire is the most casual dress code. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ)
- وصّل: Forming (التكوين) = Team comes together, roles start
- وصّل: Storming (العصف) = Conflict may emerge, disagreements natural
- وصّل: Norming (وضع القواعد) = Rules and expectations become clear
- وصّل: Performing (الأداء) = Team works productively
- وصّل: Adjourning (الانفضاض) = Teams disband, lessons recorded
- وصّل: Empathic Listening (الاستماع التعاطفي) = Understand feelings and needs
- وصّل: Business Casual (اللبس العملي غير الرسمي) = Collared shirts, slacks, skirts

[A) It supports every stage of collaboration, from brainstorming to finalizing details B) It can only check spelling at the end of a project C) It replaces the need for team members D) It works only as a stand-alone app, never as a copilot] ✔ A) It supports every stage of collaboration, from brainstorming to finalizing details (يساعد في كل مراحل التعاون، من العصف الذهني لين التفاصيل الأخيرة)

- Emotional intelligence means ignoring your emotions and the feelings of others. [A) True B) False] ✔ B) False (خطأ، الذكاء العاطفي هو فهم احتياجات الآخرين، ومزيج من المهارات العاطفية والاجتماعية)
